# Vault13 – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Vault13 is a private photo and video vault for iPhone and iPad, made by Speeder2k13.

## The short version
Vault13 does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Nothing you store in Vault13 is sent to us.

## Your photos and videos
- Everything you import is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it is saved.
- If you choose a backup folder, an encrypted copy is stored in your own iCloud Drive, in the folder you picked. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it, and the files cannot be opened without your vault key.

## PINs and recovery code
Your PINs, the vault key and the recovery code are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are never sent anywhere.

## Photo library access
Vault13 asks for access to Photos only to import the items you select, to save items back to Photos when you ask, and, if you turn this option on, to delete the originals after import. iOS asks you to confirm every deletion.

## Settings
Your preferences (language, sound, import quality and similar) are stored only on your device.

## Deleting your data
- Settings > Reset > "Erase the vault from this device" removes everything from the device.
- "Erase everything, including the iCloud backup" also deletes the Vault13 backup folder.
- Deleting the app removes all local data. You can also delete the Vault13 folder from iCloud Drive in the Files app.

## Children
Vault13 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

# Vault13 – Politica de confidențialitate (română)

Data intrării în vigoare: 12 noiembrie 2026

Vault13 nu colectează date. Nu are cont, analiză statistică, reclame sau SDK-uri externe. Pozele și clipurile sunt criptate AES-256 pe dispozitiv; backup-ul opțional stă criptat în folderul ales de tine din iCloud Drive, la care noi nu avem acces. PIN-urile, cheia seifului și codul de recuperare stau doar în Keychain-ul dispozitivului. Accesul la Poze e folosit doar pentru import, salvare în Poze la cerere și, dacă activezi opțiunea, ștergerea originalelor (cu confirmarea iOS). Poți șterge totul din Setări > Resetare sau ștergând aplicația și folderul Vault13 din iCloud Drive.
