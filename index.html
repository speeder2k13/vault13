Vault13 in English

A private vault for iPhone and iPad: photos, videos, documents and passwords, locked in one place. Vault13 encrypts everything on your device and backs it up to your own iCloud Drive folder.

  • Photos and videos in encrypted albums: import from Photos, Files or Safari, plus a private camera.
  • Real PIN and decoy PIN: a fake vault to show when you are made to open the app.
  • PDF documents: import, scan, folders, page editor and printing.
  • Password book for accounts, Wi-Fi, codes and notes, with the Magic card: copy a password from the vault door, for one minute.
  • Photo editor: crop, straighten, adjustments and filters.
  • Intruder log: a photo and the location of whoever enters a wrong PIN.
  • Sync between iPhone and iPad, and direct transfer with a QR code.
  • In English or Romanian. No account, no ads, no data collected.
